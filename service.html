What Does a Primary Care Provider Do?

A PCP manages routine wellness, preventive screenings, and common medical conditions for all ages.

If specialty care is needed, your PCP coordinates referrals and stays connected to your complete treatment plan.

Find a Primary Care Doctor
What is primary care?

Primary care is comprehensive first-contact care that includes prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and care coordination.

How does preventive care help?

It helps identify risk factors early through screenings and routine follow-up before problems become more serious.

Can my PCP refer me to specialists?

Yes. Your PCP can coordinate referrals and share records so specialty care aligns with your overall plan.
